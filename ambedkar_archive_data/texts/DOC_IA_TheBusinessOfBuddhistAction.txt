Title: The Business of Buddhist Action
Author: Anjali Chatterjee
Date: 2016-06-13

A talk by Anjali Chatterjee at Manchester Buddhist Centre on Triratna Night, 13th June 2016, during June’s Buddhist Action Month – BAM! Anjali talks about a potential team-based right livelihood business inspired by the Dharma. Drawing on the radical example and ideas of Dr. Ambedkhar, the business will aim to work in collaboration with the India Dhamma Trust
