Title: Constituent Assembly Debates: Debate on Uniform Civil Code (Article 44)
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1948-11-23

We have in this country a uniform civil code in almost every aspect of civil life except personal laws: we have a uniform law of crimes under the Indian Penal Code, Civil Procedure Code, Transfer of Property Act, Contract Act, and Sale of Goods Act. The only little corner which has remained untouched is personal law relating to marriage and succession.

I do not propose that the State shall immediately enforce a code upon all communities irrespective of their willingness. The article merely declares it to be the duty of the State to endeavor towards it, while providing ample room for voluntary application and evolutionary consensus.
