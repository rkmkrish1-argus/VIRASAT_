Title: Newsreel 1791
Author: Ministry of Information, Films Division
Date: 1983-01-01

Films Division Number 006550 Synopsis: 1. POLLING IN ASSAM: Unfortunately it was marred by several incidents of violence. Agitators decided to boy cot the elections. Now a total of 600 candidates are in the running for 126 Assembly seats. 2. THE NATIONAL SCENE: (a) REHABILITATION OF CRIMINALS (CHANDIGARH): Citizens Association for Relief, Education and Service (Cares) has undertaken the task of reforming and rehabilitating prisoners at the Burail jail in Chandigarh. (b) LEPROSY CONTROL PROGRAMME (HARYANA): Indira Chakravarty Gram is a home for leprosy patients on the outskirts of Karnal town. 3. FAIRS and FESTIVALS; (a) CARNIVAL IN GOA - Panaji, it symbolises the spirit of communal harmony and good cheer. The highlight of the Carnival is the colourful variety of floats led by the legendary King Memo and his courtiers. (b) 678TH URS CELEBRATIONS (DELHI): Or His Holiness Hazrat Nizammuddin Aulia. 4. SPORTS: DURAND FOOTBALL MATCH (DELHI): Ambedkar Stadium. East Bengal vs. Mohun Bagan, East Bengal win. 5. REGIONAL NEWS (NORTH): (a) EXPORT-IMPORT BANK (DELHI): Union Minister of Finance, Mr. Pranab Mukherjee inaugurates it. (b) HOUSING FOR WEAKER SECTIONS (PUNJAB): Punjab Govt. launches a massive low-cost housing scheme. Director: Not Available From the Government of India, Ministry of Information, Films Division
