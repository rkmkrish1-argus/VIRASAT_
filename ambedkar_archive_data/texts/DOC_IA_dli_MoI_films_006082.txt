Title: Newsreel 1683
Author: Ministry of Information, Films Division
Date: 1981-01-01

Films Division Number 006082 Synopsis: 1. 1981 - YEAR OF THE DISABLED: New Delhi, 5th January. India formally launches the International Year of the Disabled. Talkie: Prime Minister. An exhibition “Science serves the disabled” is put up. 2. WORLD OF SCIENCE: (a) SCIENCE CONGRESS (VARANASI): 68th session of the Indian Science Congress is inaugurated by the Prime Minister. (b) 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE IARI (NEW DELHI): Indian Agricultural Research Institute is responsible for creating a large and highly competent scientific manpower contributing to industrial growth. 3. THE NATIONAL SCENE: (a) FOREIGN MINISTER OF NORWAY VISITS INDIA (NEW DELHI): Mr Feydenlund and Mr Venkatraman, India’ Finance Minister sign a five-year agreement for promotion of socio-economic relations between the two countries. (b) BHUTAN KING VISITS INDIA (NEW DELHI): King Jigme Singhe Wangchuk is welcomed by the President and the Prime Minister. (c) HANDLOOM EXPO (CALCUTTA): National Handoom Exhibition is opened. 4. SPORTS: DURAND CUP FOOTBALL FINAL (NEW DELHI) Ambedkar Stadium. Mohun Bagan meets Mohammedan Sporting. Mohun Bagan win. 5. REGIONAL NEWS: (a) BABA AMTE A FOUNDER ANANDVAN (MAHARASHTRA) (b) FREE DENTAL CAMP (MP): Khitola Village. (c) GOLDEN SAND DUNE FEST.
