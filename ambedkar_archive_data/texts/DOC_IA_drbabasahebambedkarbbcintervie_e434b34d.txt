Title: Dr Babasaheb Ambedkar BBC Interview 1955 Exposing M. K. Gandhi & Congress
Author: BBC
Date: 1955-01-01

This audio recording contains Babasaheb Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar's interview to BBC in 1955. In the interview, Babasaheb rips apart the persona of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi. Dr. Ambedkar exposes the duality of Gandhi on Untouchability and also clarifies that It was INA & Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose who compelled the British to leave India, not the much talked "Quit India Movement" of Gandhi.
