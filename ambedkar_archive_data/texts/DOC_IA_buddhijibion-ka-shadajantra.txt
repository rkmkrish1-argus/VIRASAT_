Title: Buddhijibion Ka Shadajantra
Author: Rajiv Patel and Sanjay Kumar Singh
Date: 2022-08-28

This book discovers the real history of India. It gives us a totally different analysis of our history apart from what the Brahmin writers narrated.With historical evidences the authors proved it that Hinduism is an ancient religion.It was emerged after the 9th century of Christ era.Our ancient history is Buddhism. Hinduism was born from the womb of Buddhism, specifically it was copied from Buddhism.
