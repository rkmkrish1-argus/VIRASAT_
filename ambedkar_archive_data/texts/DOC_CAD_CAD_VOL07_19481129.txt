Title: Constituent Assembly Debates: Debate on Untouchability Abolition (Article 17)
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1948-11-29

The Constituent Assembly took up Draft Article 11: 'Untouchability is abolished and its practice in any form is forbidden. The enforcement of any disability arising out of Untouchability shall be an offence punishable in accordance with law.'

Dr. Ambedkar strongly championed the immediate constitutional prohibition of untouchability without exceptions or qualifiers. He underscored that civil equality is meaningless if social ostracism and systemic degradation are permitted under the guise of tradition. This article represents a solemn national covenant to eradicate the thousands of years of humiliation inflicted upon the depressed classes. Parliament shall have the exclusive power to enact penal laws under Article 35 to ensure stringent, uniform enforcement across all states.
