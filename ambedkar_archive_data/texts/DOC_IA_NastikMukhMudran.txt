Title: Nastik Mukh Mudran (Rare Book) - A Buddhist Reply to Ambedkarite Atheists
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1950-01-26

This book has been written as a reply to Materialistic Atheism propounded by false Buddhist Ambedkarites. The author clarifies the concepts and core teachings of Buddhism as preserved in the Pali Canon. It is a reply to those Ambedkarites who propound that Pali Canon has been corrupted by Brahmins. The Buddhist monk defends such accusations in a very humble and polite manner using right speech. This rare book has been written in Hindi by Bhante Nirgunanda Thero. There is clearly no wrong intention behind uploading this book. The clear intention is to clarify the misconception in the mind of newly converted Buddhists influenced by Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar (Hero of India).
