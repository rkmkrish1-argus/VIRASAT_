Title: Constituent Assembly Debates: Debate on Emergency Provisions and Article 356
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1949-10-14

With regard to Article 356, I do not altogether deny that there is a possibility of these articles being abused or employed for political purposes. But that objection applies to every part of the Constitution which gives discretion to the Executive.

The proper thing we ought to expect is that such articles will never be called into operation and that they would remain a dead letter. If at all they are brought into operation, I hope the President, who is endowed with this power, will take proper precautions before actually suspending the administration of the provinces.
