Title: States and Minorities: What are Their Rights and How to Secure Them
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1947-03-15

Memorandum on the Safeguards for the Scheduled Castes submitted to the Constituent Assembly on behalf of the All India Scheduled Castes Federation.

This document is a complete constitution in draft form. It proposes State Socialism: key industries, basic industries, and insurance shall be owned and run by the State; agricultural land shall be nationalized and leased to collective farms composed of all residents without distinction of caste or creed. It provides that fundamental rights must protect individual liberty against state tyranny, but also safeguard weaker sections from economic exploitation by private monopolies.
