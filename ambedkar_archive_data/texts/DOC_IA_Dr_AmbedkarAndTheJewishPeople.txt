Title: Dr. AMBEDKAR AND THE JEWISH PEOPLE
Author: Dr. Ambedkar
Date: 1950-01-26

"Fifty years ago in a special interview with the editor of a monthly Jewish publication in Bombay (The Jewish Advocate, November 1941), one of the India's most respected leaders expressed openly his admiration for the pioneering efforts of the Jews in Palestine" in respect of the Social order that was being created there " (in Palestine) as he said .. It was none other than the" Father ofIndian Constitution" and the Leader of the Depressed Classes in India, Dr. Ambedkar...........
