Title: Constituent Assembly Debates: Debate on Religious Freedom and Social Reform
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1948-12-09

The religious conceptions in this country are so vast that they cover every aspect of life from birth to death. There is nothing which is not religion. If we agree that religious freedom covers all actions claimed as religious, then the legislature would be disabled from enacting any social reform legislation whatsoever.

We must claim the right to separate religious belief and faith from secular activities associated with religion. The State must retain paramount power under Article 25(2) to regulate secular activities and provide for social welfare and reform, including throwing open Hindu religious institutions of a public character to all classes and sections of society.
