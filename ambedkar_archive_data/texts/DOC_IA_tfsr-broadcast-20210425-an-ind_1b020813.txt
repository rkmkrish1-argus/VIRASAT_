Title: An Indian Anarchist On Anti Caste Organizing
Author: The Final Straw
Date: 2021-04-25

This week we are very happy to present an interview with Pranav Jeevan P, who is a student, a writer, an anti-caste activist, and an Indian anarchist living in the state of Kerala. If you are listening to the radio edit, know that we had to cut out large portions of the original audio for time’s sake, but in this interview you’ll hear Pranav explaining how he got into anarchism, how anarchistic praxis unfolds in India, some about the origins of and worldwide implications of the caste system, anti-caste organizing and how anarchism feeds it, and about how the BJP and Hindutva have real influence on people’s lives and destinies. In the expanded interview, he touches on the state of Kashmir, the struggle of Dalit and Other Backwards Caste folks and how this tendency has always had solidarity with Black liberation here, much more information about the anti CAA protest and the Farmer’s Protest, and many many more topics. There are a lot of terms in this episode which may be unfamiliar to all listeners, and we warmly invite folks to take a look at our show notes for this episode to see many links for further reading and research.
