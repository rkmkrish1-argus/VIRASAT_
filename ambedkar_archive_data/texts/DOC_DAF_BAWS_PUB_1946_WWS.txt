Title: Who Were the Shudras? How They Came to Be the Fourth Varna
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1946-10-10

Dedicated to Jyotirao Phule, the greatest modern social reformer of Maharashtra.

Through exhaustive historical and textual analysis of Vedic literature, Dr. Ambedkar proves that the Shudras were originally Aryans belonging to the solar dynasty who had a high status, and that their degradation into a subordinate fourth varna was the outcome of a prolonged historical struggle with the orthodox priesthood over social and religious supremacy.
