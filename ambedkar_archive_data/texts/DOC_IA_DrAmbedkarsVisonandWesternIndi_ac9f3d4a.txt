Title: The Curse of Narcissus - Getting over Yourself!
Author: Arthapriya
Date: 2017-10-15

In this talk, Arthapriya provides a warm and provocative invitation to Western Triratna Buddhists to rise to the challenge of Dr Ambedkar's vision by working collectively and ultimately seeing that our own well-being and freedom is so interwoven with those around us. Given as part of   Manchester Buddhist Centre's Dr Ambedkar celebrations. 15th October 2017.
