Title: Delusions of Arun shourie
Author: Vasant Moon
Date: 1950-01-26

This book is a powerful rebuttal to Arun Shourie’s controversial work Worshipping False Gods, which harshly criticized Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and his legacy. Authored by noted Ambedkarite intellectual Vasant Moon, Delusions of Arun Shourie is not an attempt to defend Dr. Ambedkar—whom Moon calls the "brightest star shining in the galaxy of Indian heroes"—but to expose the distorted and prejudiced narratives propagated by Shourie. With clarity and conviction, Moon deconstructs the misleading interpretations, half-truths, and casteist undertones in Shourie’s writing. He presents a detailed historical background of Dr. Ambedkar’s struggles, his academic brilliance, his advocacy for the annihilation of caste, and his pivotal role in shaping modern India’s democratic and constitutional values. Key chapters address: The social and psychological roots of Ambedkar's activism The brutality of caste oppression and systemic untouchability The context and importance of the Round Table Conferences A thorough explanation of Ambedkar’s demands for separate electorates A factual narration of the Poona Pact, countering Gandhi's political maneuvering A critique of Shourie’s agenda and historical distortions Moon argues that Shourie’s portrayal of Ambedkar is not just intellectually dishonest but part of a broader effort to undermine social justice movements and re-cast history to suit upper-caste narratives. Drawing upon official records, Ambedkar’s writings, and perspectives from prominent scholars and critics of Shourie, the book stands as an important contribution to Ambedkarite literature and Dalit historiography.
