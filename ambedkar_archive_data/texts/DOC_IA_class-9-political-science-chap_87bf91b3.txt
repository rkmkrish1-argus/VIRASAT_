Title: Class 9 Political Science Chapter wise Topic wise Notes Chapter 2 Constitutional Design
Author: Artham Resources
Date: 2024-01-01

This chapter discusses the process of creating a constitution, focusing on how countries design systems of governance based on principles of justice, equality, and freedom. It highlights the significance of a constitution in establishing the fundamental rights and duties of citizens and governing authorities. The chapter explores the making of the Indian Constitution, with a special focus on the role of the Constituent Assembly and influential leaders like Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. It also examines the importance of constitutional values in building a democratic nation and ensuring fair governance for all citizens.
