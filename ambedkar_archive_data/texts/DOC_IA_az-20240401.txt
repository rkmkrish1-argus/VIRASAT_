FROM THE EDITOR'S DESK: 133TH BIRTH ANNIVERSARY OF BHIMRAO RAMJI AMBEDKAR - Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar (1891-1956 C.E.) popularly known as Babasaheb, was an Indian lawyer, politician and academic. As independent India's first law minister, he was the principal architect of the Constitution of India. He was born on April 14, 1891 into a poor Mahar family. Bhimrao Ambedkar belonged to a lower caste community; JEW TOWN, MATTANCHERRY, KOCHI, INDIA: PARADESI SYNAGOGUE - The Jewish Synagogue in the port city of Kochi is the oldest synagogue in all the Commonwealth of Nations.
