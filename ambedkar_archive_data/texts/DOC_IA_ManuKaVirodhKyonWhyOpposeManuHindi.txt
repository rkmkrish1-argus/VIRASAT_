Title: Manu Ka Virodh Kyon Why Oppose Manu Hindi
Author: Dr Vivek Arya
Date: 1950-01-26

Manu Smriti Text is widely criticized by a group of persons. They claims that Manu supported Caste System and Manu was against Shudras. This is a Myth. In this small tract this doubt is clearly explained that Manu was not a supporter of Caste System. In middle ages interpolations were done in Manu Smriti which lead to this belief.
