Title: Incongruities In Indian Constitution
Author: BS Murthy
Date: 2019-12-02

Mohammed Ali Jinnah got what he wanted for the Musalmans of Hindustan, though in time, their religious fervour turned Pakistan into a Rogue State.        What of India, the product of an irony of a partition? While the Hindu nationalists lamented about the loss of the country’s land, the  Musalman intellectuals were alarmed at their reduced numbers vis-à-vis the Hindus. Even as the Golwalkars articulated the Hindu frustration in shrill voices, the Maulana Azads voiced the Muslim fears in secular tones. Whatever, as Pakistan became an Islamic nation for the  Musalmans , India remained a habitat of varied interest groups, the  Musalmans  included! While the Indian political classes were beset with a sense of loss that partition brought in, in its wake, the Hindu intellectuals were upset by the age-old caste guilt that the reform movement occasioned in their collective consciousness.
