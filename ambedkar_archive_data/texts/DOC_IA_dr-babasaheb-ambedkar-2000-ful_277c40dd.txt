Title: Dr Babasaheb Ambedkar ( 2000) Full Movie ( Hindi)
Author: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Date: 1950-01-26

"Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar" is a 2000 Indian bilingual film directed by Jabbar Patel, featuring Mammootty in the lead role as Dr. B. R. Ambedkar. The movie delves into the remarkable life of Dr. Ambedkar, renowned for his pivotal role in championing the rights of marginalized and oppressed castes in India. His legacy is deeply intertwined with his significant contributions to shaping the Constitution of India, notably as the chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constituent Assembly. The film serves as a compelling narrative that highlights Dr. Ambedkar's tireless efforts towards social justice and his enduring impact on Indian society.
