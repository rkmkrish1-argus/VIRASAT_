Title: Ambedkar
Author: B.R Ambedkar
Date: 1950-01-26

Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar, also known as Baba Saheb Ambedkar , was an Indian jurist, economist, politician, and social reformer who played a crucial role in drafting the Constitution of India and advocating for the rights of Dalits and females and  people of India. He was born on April 14, 1891 , and is often referred to as the "Father of the Indian Constitution.
